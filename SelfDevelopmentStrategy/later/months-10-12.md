# Months 10–12 — Operating, and the decision

The thinnest file here, and thin on purpose. Its content is a *decision*, and
specifying a decision in advance is making it. A file that said today which
direction the year should end on would be the exact failure the whole apparatus
exists to prevent.

**Rewrite this once month 9 has closed.** Everything below is a frame, not a plan.

## Capability

**Operating rather than building.** No new capabilities unless a real need
demands one. The skill on trial is whether three systems keep working while you
are not adding to them — and whether the telemetry tells you before a user does.

This is the least satisfying period in the year and the one that most separates
engineers who ship from engineers who start. Nothing here demos.

**Evidence that survives a skeptical reader.** Re-read every artifact as a
stranger would. Any credibility claim that has drifted from what the artifact
actually demonstrates gets either repaired in the artifact or rewritten in the
claim — never left standing as an aspiration.

## Project milestones

All three systems in sustained operation, with the scoreboard filled weekly and
the eval gate genuinely gating. One month of that is worth more, as evidence,
than a fourth system.

If a system has not been used in a month, say so and retire it. A retired system
honestly recorded is a stronger portfolio item than a maintained one nobody
touches, because retiring it is a judgement and maintaining it is inertia.

## Business goal

Revenue if it exists. **An honest zero if it does not** — recorded, not omitted. A
scoreboard that drops its hardest metric has stopped measuring.

If consulting has traction, the work is deepening the repeatable offer. If it has
not, the work is deciding whether the channel or the niche was wrong, from the
funnel record rather than from feeling. Two readings are available and only the
data separates them: the message was wrong, or the audience was.

## Measurable checkpoint

- Three systems ran for the full quarter without a capability being added to
  justify their existence.
- Every portfolio artifact has a credibility sentence that is true as written, and
  each points at something that **runs** or a measured number with its denominator.
- The funnel's twelve-month record is complete, with `real`/`simulated` tags intact
  throughout. By now it has stopped being a target and become testimony.
- Revenue is recorded — including if it is zero.

## Decision point

**The twelve-month direction, on measured evidence.**

Two of the four options are argued directly from the funnel record: whether money
ever changed hands, and whether a channel with a *measured* cost per conversation
exists at all. Both readings are only as good as the evidence tags applied along
the way — which is why the tag was never optional, and why revenue was never
allowed to carry a simulated one.

Twelve months ago you had a preference, and it was probably strong. The whole
instrumentation of this plan — logged hours, measured reply rates, attack success
rates with denominators, a scorecard that is allowed to say no — exists so that at
this point the preference and the evidence can be told apart rather than quietly
merging.

Then write the year-two plan. It should be shorter than this one was.
